ToonRig Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

Summary

ToonRig is made by DM Creator. ToonRig itself does not collect, upload or sell any of your personal data. There is no sign-up and no account, and everything you make in it is stored only on your phone. The app uses the internet only to show ads, which come from Google — see Third parties below.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, ToonRig keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • audio recordings you import, and the lip-sync analysis made from them;
  • pictures you import as backgrounds or props;
  • characters you create, and the poses, scenes, timelines and keyframes you make;
  • the name you enter on your profile, and your app settings.

None of this leaves your phone unless you choose to share it. The analysis of your recordings is done on the device.

Files and permissions

ToonRig never asks you to grant a permission: it has no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or the files on your phone. The ads in it need internet access and your device's advertising ID, which Android allows without asking. When you import a recording or a picture, Android's own picker lets you choose the file, and ToonRig receives only the files you pick. ToonRig keeps its own copy so your work still opens if the original is moved or deleted.

Videos you export

When you export a video it is saved to your phone (on Android 10 and later, to the Movies/ToonRig folder in your gallery). If you tap Share, the video goes only to the app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy then applies to it.

Third parties

ToonRig shows ads from Google AdMob: one you can choose to watch to unlock a character, one after you save a character you have created, and one after each video you export. To choose and measure those ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, its IP address and how you interact with the ads, under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Where the law requires it, ToonRig asks for your consent first. ToonRig itself contains no analytics or tracking and shares none of your work with anyone.

Payments

ToonRig is currently free and has no purchases. If paid features or subscriptions are introduced, payments will be handled by a payment provider (such as Google Play or Razorpay). Your card or bank details would go to that provider, never to ToonRig or to us, and this policy will be updated before any payment is taken, to explain what is shared with the provider and why.

Children

ToonRig is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual scenes, recordings, characters and props in the app. Delete my account, in the profile menu, erases everything ToonRig has stored on this phone at once. Uninstalling the app does the same. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, we hold no copy that would need deleting — and none that could be recovered.

Security

Your data is kept in storage private to ToonRig, which Android protects from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on our Google Play page, with a new effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact DM Creator at help.quizapp.ldce@gmail.com.
